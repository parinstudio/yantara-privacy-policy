Last Updated: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how YANTARA: ยันต์อาถรรพ์ ("YANTARA", "the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the game.

YANTARA is a fantasy role-playing game developed by Parin Studio.

2. Information We Collect

YANTARA does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their real name, email address, telephone number, physical address, or location in order to play the game.

The game does not currently use third-party advertising, analytics, cloud save services, or online account services.

3. Local Game Data

YANTARA stores game-related information locally on the user's device in order to provide game functionality and preserve game progress.

Examples of local game data may include:

  • Player progression
  • Player level and experience
  • Hero information and progression
  • Battle progression
  • Gacha and summon-related game data
  • In-game resources and rewards
  • Game settings
  • Player name entered within the game
  • Other game progress required for the game to function

4. Save Data and Encryption

YANTARA uses local save data to preserve the player's game progress. The game may use JSON-based data storage and encryption mechanisms to help protect locally stored game data.

Automatic saving may occur while the game is being played.

5. How We Use Information

Locally stored game data is used only to provide and maintain the functionality of the game, including saving and restoring game progress.

We do not currently use the locally stored game data for advertising, behavioral profiling, or third-party analytics.

6. Data Sharing

YANTARA does not currently sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

The game does not currently integrate third-party advertising, analytics, Firebase services, Google Analytics, Google Play Games Services, login services, or cloud save services.

7. Third-Party Services

At the time this Privacy Policy was last updated, YANTARA does not require third-party online services to provide the core gameplay experience.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on the user's device until it is removed by the user or by the operating system.

Users can generally remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game, subject to the behavior of the user's device and operating system.

9. Children's Privacy

YANTARA does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through account registration or online forms.

If the game is made available to children, applicable Google Play policies and other applicable privacy requirements will be followed.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored game data, including the use of encryption mechanisms where applicable.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time when the game, its features, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
